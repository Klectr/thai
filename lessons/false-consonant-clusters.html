False Consonant Clusters

Today's pair looks just like yesterday's true clusters, but the ร never makes a sound at all. I checked this against two sources, and both give the same small set to watch for.

When the first consonant is จ, ซ, ท, ส, or ศ and ร follows it, drop the ร completely and run the tone on that first consonant alone. The cleanest example is จริง. With the ร gone it is just จ, mid class with no mark on a live syllable, so it reads "jing" in a mid tone and means true or really. You will hear that one constantly in ordinary conversation.

สร้าง works the same way, with one extra wrinkle. The ้ sits over the silent ร, but you still read it through ส, which is high class, so mai tho gives you a falling tone: "saang," meaning to build or create. It is the word sitting inside กำลังสร้าง on construction signs, so this one turns up on the street as much as on the page.

Then there is the one cell I would actually memorize. In ทราบ, dropping the ร is not enough, because ทร together sounds like "s," giving you "saap" in a falling tone, a polite word for to know. Reading it as "thaap" is the trap here. The one common exception is words like โทรศัพท์ for telephone, where โทร keeps its "th" sound, and those are best learned as whole words when you meet them.

For tomorrow morning I would just try those three in order, สร้าง, จริง, and ทราบ, and name the type before you read each one out loud. If ทร comes out as "s" without you having to stop and think, this pattern is already settling in, and I will be watching for whether it holds when these words show up inside longer passages.
